Proof Points

Three Quick Case Studies

1. Documentation Nobody Had to Ask For

  • The problem: At eRepublic, I built webinar registration pages in WordPress and connected them to Salesforce. The manual was outdated, and there was no one to train me.
  • What I did: Taught myself the system with test data. Rewrote the instructions, replaced every outdated screenshot, and matched each step to the right page in the manual.
  • The result: My updated steps were added to the official manual. The next person gets a guide that actually works.

2. Remote Support, Zero Dropped Balls

  • The problem: A fully distributed GovTech team was juggling 4 concurrent client engagements, and action items kept getting lost between calls.
  • What I did: Owned meeting logistics and follow-up, logged every action item in Salesforce, and kept documentation organized across 10 projects.
  • The result: Zero dropped action items between calls and 100% of deliverables on schedule, all through Slack, Notion, and Asana.

3. Partner Success Across 13 Counties

  • The problem: A statewide grant program spanning 13 California counties ran on a hand-graded, paper-heavy certification process. It took 30 days, and partners struggled to stay compliant.
  • What I did: Digitized the certification workflow with Excel and mail merge, then built a proactive tracking system for 15+ partner agreements with automated reminders.
  • The result: Certification cut from 30 days to 5 (83% faster). Partner compliance up 50%. Tracking errors down 10%.
