Executive Briefing System (Decision Support + 130+ Hours Reclaimed)

Challenge

  • High volume of inputs created decision fatigue and reactive scheduling.
  • Information arrived fragmented (email, docs, meetings) rather than “decision-ready.”
  • Follow-through suffered when decisions and actions weren’t tracked consistently.

Intervention

  • Implemented structured executive briefings and decision memos.
  • Created decision trackers to capture owners, deadlines, status, and next steps.
  • Aligned calendar strategy to priorities (protecting focus time and reducing low-value meetings).

Impact

  • 130+ executive hours reclaimed annually through briefing systems and scheduling strategy.
  • Faster decisions due to consistent “ask + recommendation” packaging.
  • Improved execution via clear ownership and follow-through tracking.

Enterprise-Tech Translation

  • Decision velocity (reduce cycle time to decision)
  • Operational rigor (repeatable artifacts)
  • Executive time as a constrained resource (capacity is a system)
