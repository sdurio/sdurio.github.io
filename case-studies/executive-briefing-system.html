Executive Briefing System
Decision Support + 130+ Hours Reclaimed

Briefing discipline, decision memos, and prioritization systems that reduce friction and return executive capacity.

130+Hours Reclaimed / Year
100%Quorum Maintained
The Problem

Challenge

  • High volume of inputs created decision fatigue and reactive scheduling.
  • Information arrived fragmented — email, docs, meetings — rather than "decision-ready."
  • Follow-through suffered when decisions and actions weren't tracked consistently.
  • Executives spent time searching for context instead of acting on it.
What I Built

Intervention

Briefing System

  • Structured executive briefs (objective, context, risks, recommendation, ask)
  • Pre-reads delivered one week early — not the morning of
  • Consistent format across all meeting types

Decision Support

  • Decision trackers: owner, options, due date, status, next step
  • Decision memos to package recommendations clearly
  • Escalation paths that don't create bottlenecks

Calendar Strategy

  • Protected focus time (2+ hours/week, non-negotiable)
  • Meeting deflection: "Could this be a shared doc?"
  • Quarterly calendar audits to prune low-value recurring meetings
Results

Impact

130+ Hours Reclaimed

Annually returned to executive capacity through briefing systems and scheduling strategy.

Faster Decisions

Consistent "ask + recommendation" packaging reduced time-to-decision across recurring leadership reviews.

Stronger Follow-Through

Clear ownership and tracked action items meant execution didn't fall through the cracks between meetings.
