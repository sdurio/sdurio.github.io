Audit Readiness + Controls
$75K+ Misallocations Prevented

Strengthened procurement controls, documentation systems, and process tooling to reduce misallocations and support audit-ready operations at scale.

$75K+Annual Misallocations Prevented
65%Audit Error Rate Reduced
75%Fewer Records Misfiled
The Problem

Challenge

  • High-volume procurement activity required accuracy, traceability, and compliance discipline.
  • Audit risk increases when documentation is inconsistent or hard to retrieve quickly.
  • Operational inefficiencies created cost leakage and rework across the procurement cycle.
  • 3,000+ physical records with no centralized system created retrieval delays and misfile risk.
What I Built

Intervention

Process Tooling

  • Custom procurement calculator to improve accuracy
  • Reduced processing time by 30%
  • Saved 200 labor hours annually

Audit Controls

  • Standardized documentation and audit-ready logs
  • 20–50 weekly audits of procurement records
  • Proactive issue identification before escalation

Records Digitization

  • Transitioned 3,000+ physical records to centralized system
  • Improved retrieval efficiency by 40%
  • Reduced misfile incidents by 75%
Results

Impact

$75K+ Prevented

Annual misallocations prevented via audit oversight, controls, and process integrity.

65% Fewer Audit Errors

Standardized documentation practices reduced internal audit error rates significantly.

40% Faster Retrieval

Centralized digital records system improved retrieval speed and reduced misfile incidents by 75%.
