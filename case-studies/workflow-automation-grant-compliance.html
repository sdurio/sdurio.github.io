Workflow Automation Case Study: 30 Days → 5 Days (83% Faster)

Context: Statewide grant compliance workflow spanning 13 counties.

Challenge

  • Manual compliance processing created a ~30-day bottleneck for approvals.
  • High coordination overhead across multiple stakeholders and recurring documentation needs.
  • Audit readiness required consistent, traceable records.

Intervention (What I Built)

  • Automated spreadsheets for tracking + validation.
  • Mail Merge-based generation to standardize outputs and reduce rework.
  • Standard templates to reduce variance and improve audit-quality documentation.

Tools: Advanced Excel, Mail Merge, standardized templates and documentation systems.

Impact

  • Cycle time reduced: 30 days → 5 days (83% improvement).
  • Audit readiness strengthened: clearer traceability and consistent documentation.
  • Decision velocity improved: faster review cycles for leadership approvals.

How This Maps to Enterprise Tech

  • Cycle-time reduction (removing bottlenecks)
  • Operational controls + governance (risk reduction without slowing teams)
  • Standardization (repeatable execution across stakeholders)
