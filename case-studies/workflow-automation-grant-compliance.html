Workflow Automation
30 Days → 5 Days

83% cycle-time reduction via automation and standardized templates across a 13-county statewide grant program.

83%Faster Processing
30→5Days Cycle Time
13Counties Coordinated
The Problem

Challenge

  • Manual compliance processing created a ~30-day bottleneck for executive approvals.
  • High coordination overhead across 13 counties with recurring documentation needs.
  • Audit readiness required consistent, traceable records — but the process produced inconsistent outputs.
  • No standardized templates meant every submission looked different and required manual review.
What I Built

Intervention

Automation

  • Automated tracking spreadsheets for validation
  • Mail Merge-based generation to standardize outputs
  • Reduced manual rework at every stage

Standardization

  • Statewide templates for consistent submissions
  • Audit-quality documentation as a byproduct
  • Reduced variance across 13 county submissions

Tools Used

  • Advanced Excel
  • Mail Merge
  • Standardized documentation systems
Results

Impact

83% Faster

Cycle time reduced from 30 days to 5 days — enabling faster decision cycles for leadership approvals.

Audit Readiness

Clearer traceability and consistent documentation strengthened audit outcomes across the program.

Decision Velocity

Faster review cycles reduced executive bottlenecks and improved organizational responsiveness.
