My Remote Defaults

Habits I bring to day one. Each one comes from a place I've already used it.

Written first

Decisions, updates, and handoffs live in writing, so a teammate in another time zone never waits on me to explain.

From: the 15-process transition manual, decision memos and briefings

Every action item has an owner, in a system

In Short

"How Do You Work Remotely?"

My 30-second answer

I default to writing. If a decision or action item only lives in a meeting, it doesn't exist yet, so I log it with an owner and a date right away.

I've run a $5M program across 13 counties entirely through remote channels and supported a distributed team I never met in person. What made it work was visibility: trackers people could check without asking, and materials sent a week early so meetings were for decisions.

My goal is that my executive never has to wonder where something stands.
