The Problem

Challenge

The lowest bid doesn't always win. Getting that right by hand, 1,300 times a year, is where compliance quietly slips.

  • At the California Department of Corrections and Rehabilitation, most purchases drew three bids: a major vendor, a mid-size vendor that didn't qualify for a preference, and a certified small business or DVBE.
  • State rules gave qualifying vendors a 5% preference during evaluation. Applied by hand, it was easy to miss, apply to the wrong vendor, or calculate inconsistently.
  • When an award was questioned, there was no single document showing how the winner was chosen.

        How It Works
        
From Bids to Award

      

      

        

          Step 1 · Identify
          
Vendor Number In

          
Enter the vendor number. Name and type fill in automatically, so nobody decides eligibility from memory.

        

        

          Step 2 · Price
          
Line Items to Subtotals

          
Unit prices times quantities roll up to a subtotal for each vendor, side by side.

        

        

          Step 3 · Adjust
          
Apply the 5% Preference

          
Qualifying vendors get the deduction for evaluation only. Everyone else is evaluated at their bid.

        

        

          Step 4 · Document
          
Winner + Audit Record

          
The lowest evaluated price wins, and the full math is written up for the file, including what the preference cost.
